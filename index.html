SIRSAWIT®

Waste to worth. Water to life.

Exploring oil palm empty fruit bunches (OPEFB/TKKS) as regenerable bio-adsorbents for water filtration and smart irrigation.

THE IDEA

Turn an abundant agricultural by-product into a useful material for water treatment, then connect water-quality information to smarter irrigation decisions.

WHAT'S INSIDE
  • Agricultural waste conversion using OPEFB/TKKS
  • Regenerable bio-adsorbents for water filtration
  • Smart irrigation monitoring
  • IoT monitoring with pH, TDS, and electrical conductivity sensors
  • Circular economy and sustainability principles
